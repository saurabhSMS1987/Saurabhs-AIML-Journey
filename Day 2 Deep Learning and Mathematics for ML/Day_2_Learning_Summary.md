## 📚 Three Core Learning Areas

### 1️⃣ DEEP LEARNING CONCEPTS
 
**Document:** `Day_2_Concepts_Explained.md`

**You'll Learn:**
- ✅ What is AI and why it matters
- ✅ How neurons work
- ✅ Multi-layer neural networks
- ✅ Different network types (CNN, RNN, Standard NN)
- ✅ Structured vs unstructured data
- ✅ Why deep learning is taking off

**Key Insight:** More data + More compute + Better algorithms = Better AI

---

### 2️⃣ MATHEMATICS FOR ML (Lessons 1-2)

**Document:** `Mathematics_for_ML_Lessons_1-2.md`

**You'll Learn:**
- ✅ Systems of linear equations (Ax = b)
- ✅ Vectors and matrices
- ✅ Cost functions (what we minimize)
- ✅ Derivatives (rate of change)
- ✅ Gradient descent (how networks learn)
- ✅ Partial derivatives (updating multiple weights)

**Key Formula:** `w_new = w_old - α × ∇J(w)`

- ✅ Add and drop columns
- ✅ Basic statistics and analysis
- ✅ Real-world data workflows
