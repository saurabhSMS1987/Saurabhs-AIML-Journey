- ✅ Why deep learning is taking off

---

### 2️⃣ MATHEMATICS FOR ML (Lessons 1-2)

**Topics:** 6 core concepts  
**Time:** 45-60 minutes  
**Document:** `Mathematics_for_ML_Lessons_1-2.md`

**You'll Learn:**
- ✅ Systems of linear equations (Ax = b)
- ✅ Vectors and matrices
- ✅ Cost functions (what we minimize)
- ✅ Derivatives (rate of change)
- ✅ Gradient descent (how networks learn)
- ✅ Partial derivatives (updating multiple weights)

**Key Formula:** `w_new = w_old - α × ∇J(w)`
